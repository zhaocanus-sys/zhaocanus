Yes — only anonymous crash diagnostics through Apple's built-in analytics.

No.

No.

## Export Compliance

### Does your app use encryption?
No. The app uses only standard HTTPS for App Store communication (handled by iOS itself). We do not implement any custom encryption.

### Does your app qualify for any encryption exemptions?
Yes — the app qualifies for the exemption as it only uses encryption provided by the operating system.

### ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption
**NO** (set to false in Info.plist)
