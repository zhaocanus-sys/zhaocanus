### Review Notes
AquaMark is a photo and video editing tool focused on watermarking. All features are functional on-device. 

Free users can:
- Add text watermarks to photos
- Crop videos
- Export in standard quality

Pro subscribers additionally get:
- Video watermarking
- Batch processing
- 4K export
- MD5 modification
- Unlimited exports

The MD5 modification feature appends random bytes to the end of video files to change the file hash. This is used by content creators to avoid duplicate detection on social media platforms. It does not alter video quality or content.
