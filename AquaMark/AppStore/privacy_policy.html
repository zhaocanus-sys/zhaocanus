Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

AquaMark ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Point: AquaMark processes all photos and videos entirely on your device. We never upload, access, or store your media on any server.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Browsing history

We may collect:

  • Anonymous usage analytics: Crash reports and basic usage statistics (e.g., which features are used most) through Apple's built-in analytics. This data is anonymous and cannot identify you.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status is managed entirely through Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not process or store any payment information.

2. How We Use Information

Anonymous analytics data is used solely to:

  • Improve app stability and fix crashes
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Guide future development priorities

3. Data Storage

All your photos, videos, watermarks, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers that store user content. Your watermark presets may be synced across your devices through Apple's iCloud service, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services

AquaMark does not integrate any third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. The only external service used is Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

AquaMark does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to retain. Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 12 months for trend analysis, after which it is automatically deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of anonymous analytics by disabling "Share Analytics" in your device's Settings → Privacy → Analytics & Improvements
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling AquaMark
  • Request information about any data associated with your use

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
